# Moira competitor and visual expectation research — 2026-08-17

## Executive synthesis

The strongest opportunity for Moira is not to imitate the largest tarot apps feature-for-feature. The market separates into three visual/product positions: **learning systems** such as Labyrinthos, **visual-first daily ritual products** such as Mystic Mondays, and **traditional/intentional reading products** that emphasize depth, hand-shuffling, or human guidance. Moira can occupy a differentiated fourth position: a **personal mystical conversation in Telegram**, with clear card-grounded interpretation, a cinematic ritual moment, and a shareable visual artifact that does not expose the private question.

## User expectations inferred from sources

The recurring expectations are **recognizable symbolism**, **a sense of ritual**, **clear personalized meaning**, **saved history**, **revisitable patterns**, **beautiful card art**, and **a low-friction daily habit**. The most important gap for Moira is not adding dozens of features overnight; it is making the existing result feel like a coherent encounter with a named oracle rather than a generated image followed by a text block.

The strongest visual expectation is a “threshold moment”: a user should see that something intentional happened before the reading arrives. The strongest sharing expectation is a compact, emotionally resonant image that contains cards, position meaning, and one safe takeaway without exposing the user’s private question.

## Strategic visual position for Moira

> **Moira is a night ritual in your pocket: three cards, one clear thread, and a small next step.**

The design should combine a deep nocturnal base, warm metallic ritual accents, restrained rose/magenta for reversed or shadow states, and a recurring Moira sigil based on a crescent, eye, and thread. The source card illustrations must remain unchanged. The renderer layer should add atmosphere, hierarchy, and brand ownership around them.
